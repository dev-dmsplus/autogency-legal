How to delete your data from Autosocial

Autosocial connects to Instagram through Meta's official API. You can remove your Instagram connection and delete all data we hold about you at any time.

Option 1 — From your Autosocial account

  1. Log in to Autosocial.
  2. Open Settings → Integrations and click Disconnect. This deletes the stored Instagram access token and account link immediately.
  3. To delete your whole account (profile, automations, leads and Instagram data), open Settings → Account and choose Delete account, or email us as described below.

Option 2 — From Instagram or Facebook

Remove Autosocial under Instagram → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites (or Facebook → Settings → Apps and websites). Meta then sends us a deletion request and we delete the data linked to your Instagram account within 30 days.

Option 3 — Email us

Send a request from the email address on your account to support@autosocial.io with the subject "Delete my data". We confirm the request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, password hash)
  • Instagram connection details and access tokens
  • Automations, message templates and logs
  • Leads and contacts collected through your automations

Billing records required by law are retained by our Merchant of Record for the statutory period. See the Privacy Policy for details.
